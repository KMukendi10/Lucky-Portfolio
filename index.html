About me

I am a passionate and self-driven multimedia creative with over 10 years of experience across graphic design, murals, typography, UI/UX, and community-targeted advertising. My work blends the vibrancy of township storytelling with global visual trends, having executed campaigns for brands like Corona, Telkom, Standard Bank, Mweb, and more.

My strength lies in creating work that is deeply rooted in local insight while delivering polished and impactful visuals that resonate across LSMs, from street culture to shopping malls. Whether it's art direction, lettering, digital marketing or murals, I bring ideas to life with craft, culture, and authenticity.

Typography
process

The typography design process begins by crafting or selecting compelling copy. Next, the lettering is structured and analysed, experimenting with layout and spacing. Various fonts and hand-drawn styles are explored to find the perfect blend, followed by building harmony and rhythm among different styles. The sketch is then vectorised in Adobe Illustrator, ensuring consistency and alignment. Finally, colours and textures are applied, and the design is saved for presentation or production, maintaining quality and impact.
